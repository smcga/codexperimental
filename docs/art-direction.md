# Art direction

The demo should not read as a random catalogue of effects. The engine may be maximal, but the release timeline must be curated.

## Suggested arc

1. Boot prophecy — terminal setup.
2. First proof — recognisable demoscene vocabulary.
3. Acceleration — AI-assisted abundance and overload.
4. Human curation break — calmer, more readable showcase.
5. Rap argument — accusations of slop, imitation and authorship become the subject.
6. Collapse / fake ending.
7. Final answer — synthesis rather than mere quantity.

## Pruning rules

Every release effect should justify at least one role:
- historical reference;
- AI authorship argument;
- musical hit;
- hero moment;
- transition support;
- atmosphere supporting a stronger foreground idea.

Effects that are merely “another cool thing” should be cut, shortened, or demoted to a layer.
